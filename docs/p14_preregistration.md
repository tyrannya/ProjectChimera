## 1. The question, and why this axis

> Does mechanically defined signed trade-flow imbalance, measured on one closed
> Binance spot BTCUSDT 1-minute bar, carry causal directional information about
> the next 1-minute bar beyond a no-information floor — and, only if it does,
> does that information survive the frozen 20 bps round-trip cost model?

**The cell this reads has never been read.** `P3` folded Binance's spot
`aggTrades` into *hourly* sufficient statistics and evaluated them under the
1h/6h programme; its closure says in as many words that it did not establish
anything about "shorter-horizon trade flow". `P6` fitted specialists on 1m bars
and gave them only the fourteen OHLCV columns. So the programme has measured
{1h} × {trade flow} and {fast clocks} × {OHLCV14}, and the intersection —
{1m} × {trade flow} — is empty.

**This is a change of information resolution and mechanism, not another feature
family on the same bars.** A negative `P3` does not bear on it: hourly
aggregation destroys exactly the timescale at which aggressive flow is supposed
to move price.

**No hypothesis is asserted.** The gap being untested is a reason to test it and
says nothing about which way it comes out.

### 1.1 What this checkpoint is not

- It is **not** a rescue of `P6`. P6's 1m cells were fitted on fourteen OHLCV
  columns at a six-bar horizon; neither their predictions nor their returns are
  commensurable with a one-bar trade-flow rule, and §7.4 forbids using them to
  contextualise a P14 verdict in either direction.
- It is **not** a promotion of P6's secondary logistic-regression or LightGBM
  leads. Those cells are post-selection leads on a different information set and
  nothing here promotes them.
- It is **not** the instrument question. Whether this machinery behaves
  differently when the market modelled and traded is the USD-M perpetual rather
  than spot is a genuinely separate checkpoint, and §8.3 records why it is
  deliberately not opened here.
- It is **not** `microstructure_v2`. `microstructure_v1`'s 32 columns are absent
  and none of its constants is revisited.

## 3. The external replication anchor

**One anchor.** Not a synthesis of several, and not the one with the largest
reported profit.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| citation | Silantyev, E. (2019). *Order flow analysis of cryptocurrency markets.* **Digital Finance** 1(1–4), 191–218. [doi:10.1007/s42521-019-00007-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42521-019-00007-w) |
| market studied | BitMEX `XBTUSD` perpetual contract, trade and quote data |
| qualitative claim | trade flow imbalance explains **contemporaneous** price change better than aggregate order flow imbalance does, and contemporaneous price change is linearly related to flow imbalance over large enough intervals |
| intervals studied | 1 second to 1 hour; **1 minute** is among the intervals presented |

**Why this anchor.** It is peer-reviewed, it is about Bitcoin rather than
equities, and its trade-flow construction needs only a trade tape with an
aggressor flag — which is exactly what Binance publishes.

### 3.1 What could not be read, stated plainly

**The article is paywalled and this session did not read its full text.** The
claim, the market and the interval range above are taken from the publisher's own
summary and from independent citing literature. **No numeric value from the
article is a target and none is quoted.** The algebra in §4 is therefore frozen
here as *ProjectChimera's own definition of the mechanism*, not presented as a
quotation from a source that was not read. A design that pretended otherwise
would be claiming a precision it does not have.

### 3.2 Unavoidable adaptations, fixed before any outcome

1. **Venue and instrument**: BitMEX `XBTUSD` → Binance spot `BTCUSDT`. Every
   checkpoint in this repository reads Binance BTCUSDT, and switching venue would
   change two things at once. The programme also forbids coin- or venue-switching
   as a research move.
2. **Dependent variable**: mid-price change from quote data → the close-to-close
   return of the 1m kline. Binance publishes no historical spot quote archive, so
   a mid price is unobtainable from the intended source.
3. **Order flow imbalance is not reproduced at all.** It needs book data this
   source does not carry. Only the trade-flow half is reproduced, and **no claim
   about the OFI-versus-TFI ordering is made or tested.**
4. **The anchor's claim is contemporaneous. P14's primary question is causal.**
   The anchor is therefore *not* evidence about how P14 comes out, and §7.1 uses
   it only as a control on the construction.

Each adaptation is written into the hashed payload. None may be revisited to
explain a result.

## 4. The signal — one column, no constant to search

```
tfi_ratio(t) = ( 2 * taker_buy_base_asset_volume(t) - volume(t) ) / volume(t)
```

evaluated on the bar opening at `t` **once that bar has closed**. Equivalently
`(aggressive buy volume − aggressive sell volume) / (total volume)`, bounded in
`[-1, +1]`.

| property | why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| one column, not a family | there is nothing to shop between |
| no lookback window | no window length to search |
| no clip, no z-score, no smoothing | no normalising constant to tune |
| scale-free and bounded | no five-year volume trend to detrend |
| strictly causal | it reads the bar it is stamped on and nothing later |
| `volume == 0` ⇒ `tfi_ratio = 0` | 211 such minutes exist and are excluded from every denominator |

### 4.1 The sign convention, proved rather than assumed

Binance publishes `taker_buy_base_asset_volume` as the base volume in which the
**buyer was the taker** — aggressive buying. The complement,
`volume − taker_buy_base`, is aggressive selling. This is the same convention the
committed `P3` trade snapshot records for `aggTrades` (`is_buyer_maker = false` is
an aggressive BUY), and it is the convention that reconciles the two archives
exactly: see §9.2 and
[`artifacts/benchmark/btc_p14_source_preflight/`](../artifacts/benchmark/btc_p14_source_preflight/).

### 4.2 The direction is fixed here

**A positive `tfi_ratio` is predicted to precede a positive return**, matching the
anchor's sign. Discovering after the fact that the opposite sign would have worked
is not a result; §10 forbids the flip.

## 5. Clock, horizon, target

### 5.1 Clock: `1m`

Fixed ex ante by three converging reasons, none of them an outcome:

- the anchor presents a 1-minute interval;
- `1m` is the native resolution of the committed source every clock in this
  repository is cut from;
- `1m` is the fastest clock `P6` established fold geometry for.

No other clock is evaluated, and no clock is chosen from a result.

### 5.2 Horizon: one native bar

The anchor's mechanism is defined at the *interval* scale. Extending it to six
bars would test **persistence**, which the anchor does not claim. This
deliberately departs from the six-native-bar convention `v4` through `P6-EXT`
used, and the departure is fixed here rather than discovered later. Horizon
families are `P9` and are forbidden here.

### 5.3 Target

One definition of "the return of bar `k`", used by every stage so that the
contemporaneous control and the causal gate cannot drift apart:

```
r(k) = close(k) / close(k-1) - 1
```

which is the return realised **over** bar `k`, since the bar opening at `k` spans
`[k, k+1min)` and `close(k-1)` is the last trade before it. §7.1 pairs
`tfi_ratio(t)` with `r(t)` — the same bar. §7.2 pairs `tfi_ratio(t)` with
`r(t+1)` — the next bar, which is the only causal pairing. The target is `r(t+1)`.

**There is no three-class label and no class threshold.** Introducing one would
add a constant the anchor does not fix. The target enters the primary gate through
its *sign* and the economic screen through
`nn.evaluate.realised_trades`.

Excluded, by rules fixed here:

- a decision bar whose immediately following minute is absent from the source —
  the segment discipline `nn.data_pipeline` already applies, so no target crosses
  a market-data gap;
- a decision bar with `r(t+1)` exactly `0`;
- a decision bar with `tfi_ratio(t)` exactly `0`;
- the last decision bar of each block, which has no successor.

## 7. The three stages

### 7.1 Stage 0 — mechanism-presence control

A check that has never failed is not evidence. This one can fail, and if it does
the checkpoint **forfeits** rather than reporting a verdict.

**Decision set**: `D0 = { t in the outer block : bar t exists, tfi_ratio(t) ≠ 0,
r(t) ≠ 0 }`. The statistic and its baseline are both measured over `D0`.

**Statistic**: the *contemporaneous* agreement rate between `sign(tfi_ratio(t))`
and `sign(r(t))` — the return realised over the **same** bar the flow was measured
on — over `D0`, per outer block.

**Baseline**: the same block's best constant-direction rule over `D0`,
`max(#{t ∈ D0 : r(t) > 0}, #{t ∈ D0 : r(t) < 0}) / |D0|`.

**Pass condition**: contemporaneous agreement exceeds that baseline in **all four**
outer blocks.

This is the anchor's own claim, and it is the only part of the anchor P14 expects
to reproduce. If aggressive buying does not coincide with the price rising in this
data, then the construction, the sign convention or the source is wrong and no
causal statistic computed on top of it means anything.

**It is not evidence about alpha.** Same-bar agreement is not available at the
decision instant. It may never enter a model, a feature, a threshold or a trading
rule, it cannot make P14 positive, and it is reported as a control.

**On failure**: P14 terminates `NOT EVALUABLE`, screen-wide. The disposition is
explicitly ambiguous between a construction defect and a genuine absence of the
mechanism in this market, and an independent reviewer must settle which before any
re-run. Stage 1 is not reached and no causal statistic is computed.

### 7.2 Stage 1 — the causal predictive gate (primary)

**The decision set**, fixed once so that the statistic and its baseline are
measured over exactly the same rows:

```
D = { t in the outer block : bar t exists,
                             bar t+1 exists and is the immediately next minute,
                             tfi_ratio(t) != 0,
                             r(t+1) != 0 }
```

**Statistic and baseline**, both over `D`, per outer block:

```
A = #{ t in D : sign(tfi_ratio(t)) == sign(r(t+1)) } / |D|

B = max( #{t in D : r(t+1) > 0}, #{t in D : r(t+1) < 0} ) / |D|
```

`B` is the best a constant-direction rule could have done on that block. It is a
**hindsight floor, deliberately the hardest fair one**, in the spirit of P7's
fold-wise-best constituent benchmark — chosen precisely so that "beats a coin"
cannot be reported as "carries information". Sharing `D` between the two is what
stops a difference in exclusions from being mistaken for a difference in skill.

**Conditions, both required:**

1. `A > B` in at least **3 of the 4** outer blocks;
2. `mean(A − B)` across the four blocks `> 0`.

**There is no model.** No family, no fit, no seed, no hyperparameter: the
statistic is a sign agreement over published arithmetic. This is the *direct
statistical signal test* option, chosen over a logistic regression because it
removes the model-family choice entirely — and because the one interpretable
family this repository might otherwise have reached for has post-selection leads
attached to it on this very clock.

**What passing Stage 1 does NOT mean, stated before it can be misread.** Each
outer block holds on the order of 10⁵–10⁶ decision rows, so an edge far too small
to pay 20 bps would still clear `A > B`. Stage 1 is deliberately a **permissive
filter**, not a finding: its only job is to stop a dead signal from reaching the
cost model. A Stage 1 pass is **not** evidence of alpha, is **not** reportable as
a positive result on its own, and licenses exactly one thing — running Stage 2.

**On failure**: P14 is **negative** and the economic screen is **never run**. A
signal that cannot beat a constant direction does not get a second chance at a
cost model.

### 7.3 Stage 2 — the economic screen, reached only through Stage 1

**Stage 2 runs if and only if Stage 0 passed and Stage 1 passed.** There is no
branch in which a failed predictive gate is followed by an economic evaluation,
and no reading of this document licenses one.

**Rule**: `LONG` if `tfi_ratio(t) > +θ`, `SHORT` if `tfi_ratio(t) < −θ`, else
`HOLD`. Entered at the close of bar `t`, held for exactly one bar, with
non-overlapping trades resolved by `nn.evaluate.realised_trades` — the single
definition in this repository of what trades a signal took and what each netted.

**Threshold**: `θ ∈ {0.05, 0.10, …, 0.95}`, nineteen predeclared points. Selected
on the **inner** block only, maximising net return after the round-trip cost, with
`min_trades = 10` — the objective and floor every prior cell used. One `θ` per
fold, on that fold's own inner block.

**Costs**: `fee_rate = 0.0005`, `slippage_rate = 0.0005`, round-trip
`cost_threshold = 0.002`, applied once per realised trade. Identical to `v4`,
`P2a`, `P2b`, `P2c`, `P3`, `P4`, `P5`, `P6` and `P6-EXT`. **P14 introduces no new
cost model**, and a cost model expressed per trade does not become cheaper because
trades are shorter. The instrument is spot and the position is unlevered, so there
is no funding cashflow, no margin and no liquidation to account for.

**Viability gate** — `P6` §9.2, imported verbatim rather than invented now:

1. cost-aware outer net return `> 0` in at least **3 of the 4** folds;
2. **mean** outer cost-aware net return across the four folds `> 0`;
3. beats the **native 1m momentum baseline**'s outer net return in at least
   **3 of the 4** folds.

Importing a gate that was frozen before P6's first fit is what stops a gate from
being shaped around a signal that already exists.

**The momentum baseline is a competitor, not an input.** Condition 3 needs
`ema_cross` on the 1m clock, which `nn.benchmark.fit_baselines` resolves from
`chimera.features`. Computing it does **not** widen P14's information set:
`tfi_ratio` remains the only column any P14 rule reads, and no OHLCV column may
enter the signal, the threshold or the trading rule.

**Condition 3 is a weak discriminator at 1m, and that is disclosed here rather
than discovered later.** `P6`'s own closure records that on the fast clocks the
native momentum baseline returns about `-1.000` — it takes a position on the sign
of `ema_cross` at every bar and pays 20 bps a hundred times a fold — so "beats
momentum" there means "does not trade itself to death". It is a real floor and a
very low one. **A Stage 2 pass must never be reported as having cleared three
demanding conditions.** Condition 1 is what binds, exactly as it bound in P6.

**Minimum activity**: a Stage 2 **pass** requires at least **30 realised outer
trades in every one of the four folds**. A fold below the floor is flagged `THIN`
and Stage 2 is reported `INSUFFICIENT_ACTIVITY`, which is not a pass. Thirty is
three times `select_threshold`'s own floor, and it is fixed here because `P7`
realised **13 trades across four folds — one of them zero** — and the independent
audit required a minimum effective trade count to be fixed in a *new*
preregistration **before** new evidence, not retrofitted into an old one. Any such
number is arbitrary; this one is arbitrary in advance.

### 7.4 What may not be used as a control

`P6`'s `1m` cells are **not** a control for P14 and may not be used to
contextualise, rescue or discount its verdict in either direction. They were
fitted on a different information set at a different horizon under a different
execution cadence.

### 8.1 The contract

The start is **inherited** from the committed multiclock source, not chosen for

**P14 creates no new bar.** It adds one column to rows the committed 1m source
already has.

### 8.2 Why the taker-side split rather than the raw trade tape

Signed trade flow is read from the kline archive's
`taker_buy_base_asset_volume` rather than recomputed by summing `aggTrades`
`quantity` where `is_buyer_maker` is false. This is a **source** decision, taken
before any P14 statistic existed, on reproducibility and gap-surface grounds —
never on an outcome.

It is an **identity, not an approximation**, and §9.2 proves it against the
publisher's own trade tape.

Two reasons:

- the derived column then lives on exactly the rows the committed price grid
  already has, so the information family has **no gap surface of its own**. That
  is the `P13` failure mode — an archive object existing while the rows the design
  needs do not — made structurally impossible rather than merely checked;
- 142 MB of archives a reader can re-derive every feature value from, instead of
  48.75 GB and a 3.4-billion-row streaming step.

### 8.3 The instrument, and the limitation that travels with it

A `SHORT` action **is not executable on Binance spot**, and never has been in this
repository: every economic number from `v4` onward prices a synthetic long/short
spot instrument. `Futures Execution v1` exists to close that gap operationally and
is dry-run only.

**P14 does not close it and does not claim to.** The instrument question — whether
this machinery behaves differently when the market modelled and traded is the
USD-M perpetual — is a separate checkpoint that this one deliberately does not
open, because changing the instrument *and* the information set in one experiment
would leave neither attributable. The right order is to establish an edge and then
execute it on the instrument that can hold it.

### 9.2 The sign convention, proved

Three days were fixed before the check ran — one per timestamp era and one
mid-history — and for each, the daily `aggTrades` archive and the daily 1m kline
archive were both fetched, both checksum-verified, and compared minute by minute.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| days | `2020-01-15`, `2023-06-15`, `2025-03-14` |
| archives checksum-verified | 6 of 6 |
| aggregated trades read | **2,694,256** |
| minutes compared | **4,320** |
| minutes where `volume` agrees | **4,320** |
| minutes where `taker_buy_base` agrees | **4,320** |
| tolerance / max relative difference | `1e-8` / `1.3e-14` |
| minutes present in only one source | **0** |

Summing `aggTrades` `quantity` where `is_buyer_maker` is **false** reproduces
`taker_buy_base_asset_volume` on every minute checked, to float64 summation noise,
in both eras. That establishes three things at once: the kline field is the
aggressive-buy volume, `is_buyer_maker = false` is an aggressive BUY, and reading
the signal from the kline archive changes nothing about what the number means.

### 9.3 If a future re-acquisition fails this preflight

Stop. Do not switch venue, substitute a mirror, interpolate a row, synthesise a
series, drop a period after seeing it, or relax the boundary, the span or the
column set. A failed preflight is a `NOT EVALUABLE` source disposition, not a
licence to re-specify the design against whatever data is reachable.

## 10. Forbidden once any valid P14 result exists

- flipping the sign convention because the opposite direction would have worked;
- changing the clock, the horizon, the target or the exclusion rules;
- changing the signal definition, or adding a window, a clip, a z-score or a
  second column;
- changing the `θ` grid, the threshold objective, the cost model or any gate
  condition;
- changing the minimum-activity floor after seeing a trade count;
- adding a model family, a second signal, regime conditioning or adaptive
  retraining;
- running the economic screen after a failed predictive gate, under any pretext;
- switching to the `aggTrades` tape, another venue, another symbol or another
  market because the result disappoints;
- re-reading the `P6` secondary logistic-regression or LightGBM cells as support;
- opening `P8`, reading `P4-HOLD`, or approaching Styx;
- writing a `tradeflow_v2` in the task that produced v1's result.

An amendment is legal **only before the first statistic** and **only as its own
commit**, with its reason recorded and the superseded hash kept — the pattern
`P13`'s A1/A2/A2R1/A2R2 established.

| `P14 PREDICTIVE GATE PASSED, ECONOMIC SCREEN FAILED: NEGATIVE ON TRADABILITY` | the mechanism carries information that does not survive 20 bps |
| `P14 PREDICTIVE GATE PASSED, ECONOMIC SCREEN INSUFFICIENT_ACTIVITY: NOT EVALUABLE` | a fold fell below the 30-trade floor |
| `P14 PREDICTIVE GATE PASSED, ECONOMIC SCREEN PASSED: EXPLORATORY CANDIDATE` | a candidate, and nothing more — see §12 |

Three of these six are informative in different ways, which is the point of
splitting the stages: "there is no information", "there is information that costs
eat", and "there is information that survives costs" are three different findings
and the design can tell them apart.

## 12. Evidence ceiling

**Exploratory and adaptive, and more so than any checkpoint before it.** These are
the same four real-world blocks `v4`, `P2a`, `P2b`, `P2c`, `P3`, `P4`, `P5`, `P6`,
`P6-EXT` and `P7` have already read. No P14 result is confirmatory.

A positive P14 is a **candidate and nothing else**. It would still have to survive

```
frozen architecture
  -> sustained genuinely-future paper validation
  -> mature-system freeze
  -> a separate decision about whether the hindsight-era-capped Styx adds anything
  -> only then a separately authorised very small live allocation
```

A negative P14 needs no discounting, which is the asymmetry that makes negative
results the cheap ones to trust.

## 13. Safety

- **no real money**, and no live allocation, tiny or otherwise;
- no authenticated order route is created, enabled or approached;
- **no leverage above 1x** is contemplated anywhere in this design — the
  instrument is spot and the position is unlevered;
- **Aegis remains the sole risk authority**;
- **`P4-HOLD` stays retired and unread; Styx stays sealed.**

## 14. Multiplicity and reporting

- one signal, one clock, one horizon, one sign, one primary gate;
- no model-family tournament, no second signal, no feature family, no second
  venue;
- every fold's Stage 0, Stage 1 and Stage 2 number is published whatever it says;
- there is no best-fold line, no best-threshold line, and no summary row that
  hides a fold.

## 15. Research classification

A two-stage screen of one externally anchored mechanism, whose every number is
published whatever it says. P14 selects nothing for deployment; a signal that
clears both gates has earned a candidacy, not a claim of profitability.
