## 1. The question

> Can a causal automatic router choose among eligible `SCALPING`,
> `DAY_TRADING` and `SWING` modes in a way that provides robust cost-aware
> incremental value over fixed-mode operation?

## 2. Why it is preregistered now and not opened

The design is committed **before** any evaluation because that is the only moment
at which a routing rule can be fixed without being fitted to the answer. It is
not opened because its precondition is unmet:

**No mode is currently eligible.** `P6` found none of `1m`, `5m`, `15m`, `30m`,
`1h` viable; `P6-EXT` found neither `4h` nor `1d` viable; `P7` found neither
measured consensus supportive. A router that chose among zero eligible modes
would be choosing among nothing, and a router evaluated on ineligible modes would
be evaluating something the programme has already declined to trade.

P8 opens when at least two modes are eligible under
[`trading_modes_v1.md`](trading_modes_v1.md) §2. Until then this document is a
commitment, not a result.

## 3. What the router may read

Only quantities **causally available at the decision timestamp**, and only frozen
ones:

- realised volatility state;
- ATR / range state;
- trend efficiency and persistence;
- directional consistency;
- current cross-timeframe Pythia agreement;
- calibrated specialist confidence;
- disagreement between fast and slow specialists;
- expected edge after the **frozen** transaction-cost model;
- turnover burden;
- liquidity, spread and slippage proxies available at that instant.

**No new predictive feature family may be introduced to make the router look
better.** A router that needed one would be a different checkpoint.

## 4. What the router may never read

Forbidden as a selection input, permanently:

- recent realised mode PnL used as a winner selector;
- outer-fold performance;
- backtest rank;
- post-hoc best timeframe;
- post-hoc best horizon;
- any knowledge of which mode wins the current period.

`chimera.modes.assert_no_profit_input` already enforces the run-time half of this
against the scaffold's selection path, and any P8 router joins that check.

## 5. The router's contract

At every decision timestamp it produces:

```
mode            SCALPING | DAY_TRADING | SWING | FLAT
mode_confidence
eligible_modes
reason_code
expected_cost
expected_edge
consensus_state
```

Deterministic given identical input state.

**`FLAT` is a first-class successful outcome.** The system must remain `FLAT`
when no eligible mode has sufficient frozen-model confidence, when cost-adjusted
edge is not positive, when specialists materially disagree, when required context
is unavailable, or when Aegis rejects the intended exposure.

**The router does not bypass Aegis.** The chain is: timeframe specialists →
per-mode Pythia consensus → AUTO router → Aegis → Hermes → futures dry-run venue
→ Argus.

## 6. Safety, unchanged by any router

The router may not: dynamically increase leverage; switch instrument family;
enable margin borrowing; switch exchange; choose a different coin because BTC is
difficult; open multiple contradictory modes simultaneously; override emergency
flatten; override a reconciliation dispute; or override an Aegis veto.

**One active directional mode at a time in v1.** Transitions are explicit and
observable, and follow `chimera.modes.plan_mode_transition`: a mode change with an
open position flattens and reconciles before the new mode may act.

## 7. The comparison, fixed before any evaluation

On the same timestamp universe and accounting semantics, per outer fold:

1. always-`SCALPING`, when eligible;
2. always-`DAY_TRADING`, when eligible;
3. always-`SWING`, when eligible;
4. the AUTO router.

`FLAT`/cash is included as a reference.

```
best_fixed_mode_return = max over the eligible fixed-mode controls, within the fold
AUTO_delta             = AUTO_return - best_fixed_mode_return
```

The fixed-mode benchmark is the **fold-wise maximum**, computed mechanically
inside each fold. Comparing AUTO against whichever fixed mode looked best over the
whole experiment, without accounting for that selection, is forbidden.

## 8. The decision rule

Supportive adaptive evidence requires **all three**:

1. `AUTO_delta > 0` in at least **3 of the 4** temporal folds;
2. **mean** `AUTO_delta > 0`;
3. AUTO does not obtain its advantage through materially larger turnover or cost
   exposure — the **turnover guard** below.

### 8.1 The turnover guard, frozen here

> AUTO's total realised turnover across the four outer folds may not exceed
> **1.25×** the turnover of the fold-wise best fixed-mode control it is being
> compared against, summed over the same folds.

A router that beats fixed-mode operation by trading materially more has not shown
that routing helps; it has shown that turnover was mispriced. `1.25` is fixed
here, before any evaluation, and may not be moved afterwards.

## 9. Result states

Exactly one of:

- `P8 AUTO MODE ROUTER: SUPPORTIVE ADAPTIVE`
- `P8 AUTO MODE ROUTER: NEGATIVE`
- `P8 AUTO MODE ROUTER: INVALID`
- `P8 AUTO MODE ROUTER: NOT OPENED`

**The current state is `NOT OPENED`.** A negative result, once obtained, stays
negative, and `auto_router_v2` is not written in the task that produced `v1`'s
result.

## 10. Telemetry

Bounded-cardinality only, as `trading_modes_v1.md` §7 already requires: selected
mode, eligible-mode mask, transitions, dwell time, `FLAT` fraction, router
confidence **bucket**, consensus/disagreement state, Aegis vetoes by mode, and
turnover, fees, funding, slippage, exposure, drawdown and reconciliation errors by
mode. Never a raw free-text reason, an order id, or an unbounded symbol.

## 11. Forbidden once any valid P8 result exists

- changing any router input, or adding a feature family to improve it;
- changing the fixed-mode controls or the fold-wise best-fixed-mode benchmark;
- changing any of the three decision conditions, or the `1.25x` turnover guard;
- reporting AUTO against a fixed mode chosen after seeing the experiment;
- writing `auto_router_v2` in the task that produced `v1`'s result.
